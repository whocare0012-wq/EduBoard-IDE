# Community Code of Conduct

EduBoard IDE is intended for teachers, students and developers. We expect a respectful, inclusive, learner-friendly community.

## Expected behavior
- Discuss ideas and code constructively.
- Be considerate of beginners, volunteers and people from different backgrounds.
- Protect the privacy of minors; never post student names, credentials, or sensitive classroom data.
- Give proper credit for third-party work and documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing another person's private information.

## Reporting
For community-conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing another person's private details. For software vulnerabilities see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
